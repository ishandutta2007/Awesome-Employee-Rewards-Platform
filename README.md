# Awesome-Employee-Rewards-Platform

## Top Employee Rewards Platforms Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Peer Recognition, Reward Catalogs, Points-Based Incentives & Culture Building*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Employee Rewards**. These tools help organizations build cultures of appreciation through peer-to-peer recognition, points-based reward systems, and global reward catalogs that motivate and retain talent.



**Examples** include Awardco, Bonusly, Achievers, Workhuman, Guusto, Nectar, Mo, Kudos, Assembly, and Reward Gateway (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Employee recognition and rewards has a **developing open-source ecosystem**. While proprietary SaaS dominates the enterprise market, several modern open-source platforms have emerged—most notably **Good Job** (a production-grade, multi-tenant recognition platform with dual-balance points) and **Meeds** (a gamified engagement platform with built-in kudos). This section documents every major active project, from full recognition platforms to Slack-integrated bots and gamification tools.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Awardco](https://www.awardco.com/)**

  All-in-one employee recognition, rewards, and engagement platform built around a global rewards marketplace with native Amazon Business integration, gift cards, service awards, and swag .



- **[Bonusly](https://bonusly.com/)**

  Peer-to-peer recognition platform where coworkers give small, frequent, public bonuses tied to company values, redeemable for gift cards, custom rewards, and donations .



- **[Achievers](https://www.achievers.com/)**

  Employee recognition and rewards software focused on driving engagement and retention through social recognition, rewards, and analytics .



- **[Workhuman](https://www.workhuman.com/)**

  Enterprise employee recognition and Human Intelligence platform (formerly Globoforce). Combines social recognition with performance development to improve culture and reduce turnover .



- **[Guusto](https://guusto.com/)**

  Employee recognition and rewards platform for sending digital gifts, running peer-to-peer and top-down recognition, redeemable at 60,000+ merchant locations, prepaid Mastercard, or charitable donations .



- **[Nectar](https://nectarhr.com/)**

  Employee recognition and rewards platform with peer-to-peer and manager recognition, points-based global rewards catalog, and automated milestone celebrations .



- **[Mo](https://www.mo.com/)**

  Employee experience platform focused on recognition, celebrations, and culture building through social-style interactions.



- **[Kudos](https://kudos.com/)**

  Employee recognition software designed to engage teams through enhanced communication, collaboration, appreciation, and engagement .



- **[Assembly](https://joinassembly.com/)**

  Employee recognition, rewards, and engagement platform by Quantum Workplace. Enables peer-to-peer recognition, milestone celebrations, custom awards, and challenges .



- **[Reward Gateway](https://www.rewardgateway.com/)**

  Employee engagement and recognition platform with rewards, benefits, and communication tools.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Good Job](https://github.com/dongitran/Good-Job)**

  The most complete open-source employee recognition and reward platform. **Production-grade**, multi-tenant, and API-first. Features **dual-balance points** (Giveable monthly budget + Redeemable earned wallet), peer-to-peer kudos tied to org-defined core values, admin-managed reward catalog with stock tracking and race-condition protection, leaderboards, engagement analytics, real-time SSE feed, and route-level security with JWT auth and role-based access. Built with Spec-Driven Development (SDD) and Test-Driven Development (TDD), **170 E2E tests** across 16 spec files. Tech stack: React 18, NestJS 11, PostgreSQL 16, Redis 7, Docker, GKE on GCP. **Open source** .



- **[Meeds](https://github.com/meeds-io/meeds)**

  Open-source employee engagement platform for spaces, recognition, and gamified collaboration. Employees work in collaboration spaces (team, project, or topic), sharing posts, files, notes, and tasks. Features **built-in quest and kudos system** for peer recognition, points for genuine participation (not just logging in), full-text search across all content, and profile pages aggregating activity and contributions. Java platform with database and search index, deployable via Docker or cloud images with hardened builds and unique per-VM credentials. **Open source** .



- **[Nexus Intranet Dashboard](https://github.com/praveen-sripati/nexus)**

  Open-source React intranet dashboard kit with an integrated **Kudos Recognition System**. Features peer-to-peer appreciation with real-time updates, employee directory with searchable profiles, team calendar (birthdays, anniversaries, events), company announcements with priority levels, and customizable drag-and-drop dashboard cards. Built with React, TypeScript, Shadcn/UI, and Tailwind CSS. **Frontend template** — designed as a starter kit for building custom internal tools with a polished UI .



- **[Employee Rewards and Recognition System](https://github.com/Ayesha24banu/Employee-Rewards-and-Reconigtion-system)**

  Web-based peer recognition platform where employees reward each other with points. Features leaderboard and performance tracking, rewards redemption for incentives. Python Flask backend, MySQL database, HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend. **CC BY-NC 4.0** — personal and educational use only, commercial use prohibited without permission .



- **[dekka_reward_management](https://github.com/Sanskar-Technolab-Pvt-Ltd/dekka_reward_management)**

  Reward management app built with **Frappe and React**. Enables businesses to manage employee rewards, track achievements, and automate incentive programs. Frappe framework provides built-in admin UI, role-based permissions, and REST API. **Open source** .



- **[Pulse HR](https://www.visalytica.com/tool/pulse-hr)**

  Open-source HR platform centered on employee sentiment and well-being. Emphasizes recognition and growth over pure productivity metrics. People-first approach collecting data on how employees feel and perform. **Currently in beta** — limited features and stability, no extensive integrations. **Open source** .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Peer-Reward Systems**: **PeerLy** (Go-based peer-reward with high-5's, 9 stars), **Gitlab-Reward-System** (badges based on GitLab statistics) .

- **Slack Recognition Bots**: Slack Employee Recognition Bot (Next.js, free code available, integrates directly into Slack for manual recognition with personalized mentions) .

- **Power Platform Solutions**: **Employee Shoutout Management** (SPFx, React, SharePoint), **Employee Recognition App** (Power Platform for VA healthcare, Canvas apps + Power Automate + SharePoint) .

- **Gamification Platforms**: **Coordinape** (Web3 peer allocation for DAOs, open-source, on-chain reputation via CoSoul NFTs) — while DAO-focused, demonstrates peer-driven reward allocation patterns applicable to employee recognition .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Good Job** for a complete production-grade recognition platform with dual-balance points and reward catalog, **Meeds** for gamified engagement with spaces and kudos, **Nexus** for a React intranet dashboard with integrated recognition feed, and **dekka_reward_management** for Frappe-based reward management. Add **PostgreSQL** for persistence and **Docker** for deployment.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Employee rewards platforms handle sensitive employee and compensation data; ensure compliance with data protection regulations and internal policies.

- **Open-source reality**: **Good Job** stands out as a production-grade open-source recognition platform with comprehensive features . **Meeds** provides a mature gamified engagement platform with kudos . However, most open-source options lack the global reward catalogs (Amazon Business, gift card networks) and compliance infrastructure of commercial platforms like Awardco, Bonusly, and Workhuman. Expect to integrate third-party reward fulfillment or negotiate directly with merchants for catalog-based rewards.
